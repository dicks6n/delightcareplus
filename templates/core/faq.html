Are your products genuine?

Yes. Every batch we stock is logged with its batch code and expiry date. You can check the code on your box on our Verify Product page.

How much is delivery?

It depends on your town, whether you pick up from a station or want door delivery, and the parcel weight. You'll see the exact price in your bag before paying. See all delivery prices.

How do I pay with M-Pesa?

Choose M-Pesa at checkout. We send a payment prompt to your phone — enter your PIN and you're done. The page updates automatically when the payment arrives.

Can I pay on delivery?

{% if shop.pay_on_delivery %}Yes — choose “Pay on delivery” at checkout and pay the rider with cash or M-Pesa when your parcel arrives.{% else %}Not at the moment. Please pay with M-Pesa or Airtel Money.{% endif %}

How do Glow Points work?

Members earn 1 Glow Point for every KES 100 spent, credited when the order is delivered. Each point is worth KES 1 and can pay up to 20% of a future order.

What are refill reminders?

For supplements and teas, we know how many days one pack lasts. A few days before yours runs out, we send a short email with a one-tap reorder link.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, until it's packed and shipped. Open the order from “My orders” (or Track order) and tap Cancel. If you'd already paid, we refund you.

Are supplements safe for me?

Our supplements are food supplements, not medicines. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition or take medication, please check with your doctor before starting a new supplement.
