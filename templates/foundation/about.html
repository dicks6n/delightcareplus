About the Foundation

Wellness for everyone, not just our customers.

Delight Care Plus helps people across Kenya look after their health. The Foundation is how our community helps the people who can't afford care right now: a hospital bill, school fees, or a project that keeps a whole neighbourhood healthy.

Health first

Most fundraisers cover medical treatment, medicines and recovery, the area our team knows best.

Reviewed by people

Our team reads every fundraiser before it goes live and may ask the organiser for documents. Fundraisers marked Verified have had their documents checked.

Easy to give

Give with M-Pesa in a few seconds, or with Airtel Money. You get a receipt by email if you leave your address.

Questions people ask

Who can start a fundraiser?

Anyone with a Delight Care Plus account. You can raise money for yourself, a family member, a friend or a community group. The Foundation team reviews it before it goes live.

How does the money reach the beneficiary?

Donations are collected by the Foundation. The team then pays the hospital, school or supplier directly where possible, or the beneficiary after checking the details. Contact us if you'd like to know how a specific fundraiser was paid out.

Can I give without my name showing?

Yes. Tick "Don't show my name publicly" when you donate and you'll appear as "Anonymous".

What if a fundraiser goes over its goal?

It can keep taking donations until it is closed. Any extra goes to the same beneficiary's needs, or to another Foundation fundraiser if the organiser agrees.

My M-Pesa prompt didn't arrive

Check your number and that your phone is on, then press "Resend" on the payment page. If money left your account but the page didn't update, chat with us and share the M-Pesa code.

Ready to make a difference?

Every shilling counts, and sharing a fundraiser helps as much as giving.
