Tips for a strong fundraiser
  • Use a clear title, e.g. "Help Amina get her kidney transplant".
  • Explain who needs help, what happened and exactly what the money pays for.
  • Set a realistic goal that matches the bill or budget.
  • Add a bright photo (landscape works best).
  • Post updates often, so donors can see the difference they're making.
